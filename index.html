    5. 05

      CrowdGuard

      PythonYOLOv8OpenVINOPyTorchscikit-learnOpenCVFastAPIWebSocketsReactTailwind

      A real time crowd monitoring system that counts people from CCTV, webcam or phone cameras, predicts when a crowd is turning risky, and alerts organisers before it becomes dangerous. I started it on my own after reading about stampedes at events like the Kumbh Mela.

      Code: CrowdGuard (opens in a new tab)

      How I know it works

      Trained on the 2000 frame Mall dataset with a time based split: a count corrector cuts the error from 4.34 people per frame (raw YOLO) to 1.71, the risk model reaches 86% accuracy, and a panic detector catches 98% of panic frames in simulation with zero false alarms on real footage. Accounts with roles, drawable zones, multiple cameras, and Telegram and email alerts.

      The hardest part

      Making it fast enough to watch live. Each frame took 774 ms on a 2 core CPU. An OpenVINO copy of YOLO, optical flow at half resolution and single pass models brought it down to 310 ms, with the same accuracy.
